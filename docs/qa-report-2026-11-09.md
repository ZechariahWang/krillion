# Krillion QA report, 2026-11-09

Full-game audit: four parallel code reviews (server match flow, client match UI, economy and data, lobby and
ambience) plus a live Studio play-test of several solo-with-bots matches. `lune run tests/run.luau`: 240 passed,
0 failed. Every High finding below was re-verified by hand against the source before it was kept. Items the
reviewers could not fully confirm are marked **Unconfirmed**.

Paths are relative to `src/`. `Client/` means `StarterPlayer/StarterPlayerScripts/Client/`, `Server/` means
`ServerScriptService/Server/`.

## Summary

No Critical issues, no runtime errors or warnings in a live session. The game plays end to end: fade, teleport,
countdown, prompts, reveal, surge, elimination, results and back-to-back matches all work, with clean console
output on both Client and Server.

## High

### SRV-1. Winner is "Nobody" when the last players sink in the same surge

- Where: `Server/RoundService.luau` `runSurge` and the cull loop in `start`; `Server/WaterService.luau`
  `surgeTo`; `ReplicatedStorage/Shared/MatchState.luau` `cull` and `winner`.
- What happens: `runSurge` sets the state water to its target and then `surgeTo` yields about 2.4 s while the
  live level climbs. The 10 Hz poll runs during Surge and eliminates any player whose root is under the live
  level through `eliminate`, which never touches `lastCulled`. The state `cull` afterwards only catches heights
  in roughly `[target - 3, target)` and overwrites `lastCulled` with that list, usually empty. `winner` falls
  back to `lastCulled` when nobody is alive. Late-round surges are 5 to 11 studs, so both finalists usually go
  by the poll and the podium reads "Nobody": no `addWin`, no 100-shell bonus.
- Fix: stop the Surge-phase poll from deciding the winner. Either take "Surge" out of the poll's phases and
  only eliminate on a missing root there, leaving `MatchState.cull` to decide; or clear `lastCulled` at the
  start of `runSurge`, append to it from `eliminate` while the phase is Surge, and make `cull` append rather
  than overwrite. Add a Lune test where two players are both more than 3 studs under the target.
- Related (Low): `lastCulled` persists from the previous surge, so if the alive count drops from 2 to 0 inside
  one poll during a Prompt (two resets), a player sunk a round earlier can be named winner. Clearing
  `lastCulled` per prompt covers it.

### CLI-1. Phone players who sink cannot walk in the lobby

- Where: `Client/Hud.luau` `onEliminated` and `onState` (`setTouchControls`).
- What happens: `onState` only toggles touch controls when `payload.playing` differs from the local `playing`.
  `onEliminated` sets `playing = false` without re-enabling, so the next tick sees `false == false` and never
  calls `controls:Enable()`. Sinking teleports without a respawn, so nothing else re-enables them. The player
  stands in the lobby unable to reach the shop, the quiz arc or the stairs until Results arrives.
- Fix: call `setTouchControls(true)` in `onEliminated`, or track the last applied control state separately
  from `playing`.

### ECO-1. The leave-save has no retry and shutdown waits a fixed 3 s

- Where: `Server/DataService.luau` `unload`, `BindToClose`, `save`.
- What happens: `unload` calls `save` once, then nils `sessions[player]`. When that `UpdateAsync` fails
  (throttle, outage), `save` puts the delta back into the session that `unload` then throws away: up to one
  autosave interval of Shells, quest rewards, chest drops and receipts already acknowledged with
  `PurchaseGranted`. `BindToClose` spawns the unloads and returns after `task.wait(3)`; on a full server under
  throttling, saves still in flight are cut off.
- Fix: in `unload`, retry `save` with backoff (3 tries) and only clear state after success or the last try. In
  `BindToClose`, count pending unloads and wait until the count is 0, up to the 30 s Roblox allows.

### ECO-2. A failed load looks like a wipe

- Where: `Server/DataService.luau` `readStored` and `load`; `Client/Tutorial.luau` first-visit check.
- What happens: when `GetAsync` fails, `readStored` returns `Profile.new()` with `ok = false` and `load`
  still marks the player loaded. The client gets `cash = 0`, nothing owned and `tutorialDone = false`, so the
  tutorial replays and the player believes their data is gone. Today's quest progress and the lobby cap read
  empty, so finished quests pay again. Nothing is overwritten (the save merges onto the stored value) and it
  self-heals at the next autosave, but the experience is a wipe.
- Fix: retry the load (3 tries, backoff). If it still fails, either kick with "data unavailable, rejoin" or
  mark the profile read-only for the session: refuse spends, quests and lobby payouts, skip the tutorial,
  and send `loadFailed` in the payload so the UI can say so.

### LOB-1. The quiz grabs keyboard focus as you walk onto the arc

- Where: `Client/LobbyQuiz.luau` `keepFocus`, called from `onPrompt`, `skip` and the wrong-answer path.
- What happens: on PC, walking onto the semicircle holding W captures the TextBox, types "www" and stops the
  character; the player must click out or press X to move again. Prompts auto-advance every 20 s, so the grab
  repeats. On touch, the on-screen keyboard pops up just from crossing the arc.
- Fix: do not capture focus on the first prompt of a visit, and never auto-capture on touch. Capture only
  after the player clicks the box or presses Enter; keep `keepFocus` for after a check or wrong answer.

## Medium

### SRV-2. Paid revive lost if the match ends during the pillar glide

- Where: `Server/RoundService.luau` `restore`, `onReviveUse`, `onReviveBought`; `MapService.glidePlatformTop`
  blocks 0.8 s.
- What happens: `onReviveUse` deducts the stocked revive before `restore`; Revive Now has already charged
  Robux. `restore` glides for 0.8 s before `MatchState.revive`, so the player is not alive during the glide.
  If `matchOver()` fires in that window (the cull takes the other player), `endMatch` clears the platforms and
  `restore` returns early with no refund.
- Fix: call `MatchState.revive` and set `platforms[player]` before the glide so the match cannot end under
  them, or refund (`addRevives(+1)`, clear `reviveUsed`) on the early-return path.

### SRV-3. Dead characters count as alive

- Where: `Server/PlayerService.luau` `hasCharacter` (checks only the root); `Server/RoundService.luau`
  `readyPlayers`, `liveOffer`, the `Died` and `CharacterAdded` handlers.
- What happens, two ways:
  1. A player who resets in the last seconds of intermission still has a root, so `readyPlayers` counts them.
     `startMatch` teleports the corpse onto a pillar; the respawn's `CharacterAdded` eliminates them at once.
     They lose the match before round 1 and may get a revive offer for it.
  2. After a death on a pillar, `offerRevive` opens a 5 s window. `liveOffer` passes on the dead character, so
     REVIVE teleports the corpse back up and marks the player alive. The respawn then eliminates them again;
     `reviveUsed` is already true, so the stock revive or Second Chance is spent for nothing.
- Fix: make `hasCharacter` (or a new `isAlive`) require a Humanoid with `Health > 0`, and use it in
  `readyPlayers` and `liveOffer`. For the `Died` path, delay the revive offer until the new character exists.

### SRV-4. The answer deadline grace never applies

- Where: `Server/RoundService.luau` `runPrompt`; `Server/AnswerService.luau` `closeRound`;
  `Shared/AnswerGate.luau` `isClosed`.
- What happens: `promptCountdown` ends within 0.1 s of the base deadline and `closeRound` runs at once, so a
  lock-in sent at T-0.1 s that arrives at T+0.2 s is refused as "closed". `Answer.DeadlineGrace` (0.5 s) is
  dead config for everyone without extra time.
- Fix: `task.wait(GameConfig.Answer.DeadlineGrace)` after `runOvertime()` and before `closeRound()`, skipped
  when the prompt ended early because everyone locked in.

### SRV-5. No settle pause between the surge and the next prompt

- Where: `Server/RoundService.luau` `runSurge` then `runPrompt` in `runMatch`.
- What happens: the settled-Surge broadcast and the new Prompt arrive in the same instant, so on the client
  the close-call heartbeat, the SAFE flash, the sunk/afloat toasts and the new prompt all land together.
  `Status.announceSpecial` overwrites the "CLOSE CALL!" callout a frame later (one shared label). The reveal
  row list gets about 2.4 s on screen, 1.4 s of which is the rows rolling in. On the final surge the
  Eliminated card pops and Results hides it in the same frame; two splash sounds stack.
- Fix: add `Round.SettleSeconds` (1.5 to 2 s) to GameConfig and wait that long after the final Surge
  broadcast, before the next prompt or `endMatch`.

### SRV-6. Podium ranking ignores survival

- Where: `Server/RoundService.luau` `podium`; `MatchState.winner` tiebreaks. Seen live.
- What happens: the podium is "winner, then everyone else by points". In the live test the human died in
  round 1 with all three bots alive and landed 3rd on a 0-point tie, above a still-floating bot that was left
  off. Equal height and points fall back to `pairs` order for the winner.
- Fix: rank by alive, then elimination round (store it in `MatchState.eliminate`), then height, then points.
  Add a deterministic last tiebreak (earlier lock-in time) or show a shared win.

### SRV-7. Live prompt not cleared when a match ends mid-prompt

- Where: `Server/RoundService.luau` `runMatch` break after `runPrompt`; `runReveal` is the only place that
  clears `currentPrompt` and calls `LobbyQuizService.setLivePromptId(nil)`.
- What happens: `RoundState` keeps sending the stale `prompt`/`promptId` through Results, Waiting and
  Intermission; the lobby quiz keeps excluding it until the next match; answers locked in that prompt are
  never graded or paid.
- Fix: clear both in `endMatch`. Optionally grade and pay the final prompt before ending.

### CLI-2. Countdown digit can stay on screen through the first prompt

- Where: `Client/Transition.luau` `onState`.
- What happens: a player eliminated during Starting (reset on the pillar) receives the Prompt tick with
  `playing = false`. The GO! branch needs `playing`, the `phase ~= "Prompt"` branch is skipped, so `hideText`
  never runs and "2" or "1" sits there for about 20 s.
- Fix: when `phase == "Prompt"` and `lastPhase == "Starting"`, always `hideText` (at once when not playing);
  also hide it in the `Eliminated` handler.

### CLI-3. Settings survives the teleport and opens on a pillar

- Where: `Client/Settings.luau` has no `onState`; `Main.client.luau` never notifies it. Quests, Rewards and
  ShopUi all close themselves on `playing`.
- What happens: open Settings in Intermission and you are teleported with it still up. Status and Wallet stay
  hidden under it, and its Scrim in SharedUi (DisplayOrder 3) sits above GameUi's answer bar and swallows
  taps until closed. The gear is also still clickable mid-match.
- Fix: add `Settings.onState` that closes on `payload.playing`, or call `Windows.closeAll()` on MatchIntro.
  Hide the gear while playing.

### CLI-4. Answer box may keep focus after it hides (Unconfirmed)

- Where: `Client/Hud.luau` `onEliminated`, the non-match branch of `onState`, `keepFocus`.
- What happens: when the match ends mid-prompt or the player sinks while typing, nothing calls
  `answerBox:ReleaseFocus()`, and `keepFocus` can re-capture a frame later because it checks the box and
  bar but not `gui.Visible`. WASD then goes into an invisible TextBox. Depends on whether Roblox drops focus
  when an ancestor hides.
- Fix: release focus in `onEliminated` and the non-match branch; add `gui.Visible` to the `keepFocus` guard.

### CLI-5. Late-joining client can draw the water one surge too high

- Where: `Client/WaterFx.luau` `onState` (`enteringSurge` from the client's own `lastPhase`); same pattern
  in `Client/Tide.luau` (`waterY == lastWaterY`).
- What happens: a client whose first RoundState is the settled Surge broadcast animates from the raised
  `waterY` to `waterY + surge`. The server does not rewrite WaterSurface until the next surge, so pillars look
  flooded for a round. Tide's first/second-broadcast detection also breaks when a revive or late join
  broadcasts mid-surge, because `MatchState.surge` runs before `surgeTo` yields.
- Fix: have the server send an explicit `surging = true` on the first Surge broadcast and key both
  controllers off it; skip the animation when the client's `lastPhase == ""`.

### CLI-6. Victory screen decided by display name

- Where: `Client/Results.luau` `onMatchEnded` (`winnerName == LocalPlayer.DisplayName`).
- What happens: display names are not unique; two players with the same one both get the trophy and fanfare.
- Fix: add `winnerUserId` to the `MatchEnded` payload in `endMatch` and compare UserIds.

### CLI-7. One missing authored GUI child stalls the whole client

- Where: `Main.client.luau` init chain; `Fx`, `Hud`, `Status`, `Reveal`, `Results`, `RevivePrompt`, `Lobby`,
  `LobbyQuiz` use `Ui.screen` and `WaitForChild` with no timeout; `Client/Sfx.luau` waits forever for
  `SoundService.Krillion` at require time.
- What happens: the comment in Main says controllers wait at most 10 s, but these do not. Every
  `OnClientEvent` connection comes after the init chain, so one renamed child in Studio means no later
  controller initialises and no remote handler connects.
- Fix: give these waits a timeout and early-out like Toasts, Transition and Tide already do, or connect the
  remotes before the init chain.

### ECO-3. Game-pass purchase event trusted without an ownership re-check (Unconfirmed)

- Where: `Server/StoreService.luau` `onPassBought`.
- What happens: `PromptGamePassPurchaseFinished` with `wasPurchased = true` grants the pass for the session.
  Roblox guidance treats this event as client-influenced; a spoofed event would give VIP, 2x Shells, Second
  Chance or Boost Plus for the session, and Shells earned at 2.1x persist.
- Fix: before granting, call `MarketplaceService:UserOwnsGamePassAsync(player.UserId, passId)` and grant only
  on `true`; otherwise defer to the next `PassCheck`.

### ECO-4. No session lock: a fast server hop can double-dip (Unconfirmed in practice)

- Where: `Server/DataService.luau` load and save; `Shared/Profile.luau` merge clamps cash at 0.
- What happens: server B reads the profile before server A's leave-save commits. Shells spent in A are spent
  again in B (the clamp absorbs the shortfall), and one-time grants (Starter gift, Welcome Shells, quest
  completions, StarterPack claim, a receipt left `NotProcessedYet`) can pay twice. If `UpdateAsync` commits
  but the pcall times out, the delta is re-applied.
- Fix, cheapest first: write `activeSession = {jobId, time}` on load and clear it on unload; on load, if a
  fresh one from another JobId exists, wait and retry. Or add a per-save idempotency id in the profile.

### ECO-5. A failed PolicyService lookup locks chests for the session

- Where: `Server/ChestService.luau` policy check; `Client/ShopUi.luau` chest state.
- What happens: one throw from `GetPolicyInfoForPlayerAsync` sets `allowed = false` for the whole visit with
  no retry, and the shop says "Not available where you live".
- Fix: retry with backoff; send `chestsAllowed = nil` for "unknown" so the UI says "try again shortly".

### LOB-2. The intermission countdown is hidden while you stand on the quiz arc

- Where: `Client/Status.luau` hides under any window; `Client/LobbyQuiz.luau` registers with `Windows`.
- What happens: the quiz counts as a window, so the Status chips (phase and countdown) hide. The quiz header
  shows only `WARM-UP | x/600 TODAY`. A new player warming up gets no warning before the black fade hits them
  mid-answer.
- Fix: keep Status visible for the quiz (a `keepStatus` flag on `Windows.opened`), or show phase and seconds
  left in the quiz header.

### LOB-3. Music toggled off then on quickly stays silent

- Where: `Client/Music.luau` `stop` and `start`.
- What happens: `stop` starts a 0.8 s fade to 0. If `start` runs before it ends, the sound is still
  `IsPlaying`, so `start` does nothing; the fade finishes at 0 and skips `Pause` because `enabled` is true.
  The toggle shows ON with no music until the track ends. `onProfile` also re-applies `musicOff` and
  `musicVolume` on every profile push, which can flip a fresh toggle back or snap the slider mid-drag.
- Fix: in `start`, cancel the fade and fade back up to `volumes[sound] * volume`. Apply `musicOff` and
  `musicVolume` from the first profile only.

### LOB-4. One broken track stops the playlist (Unconfirmed, engine behaviour)

- Where: `Client/Music.luau` advances only on `Ended`.
- What happens: a track that fails to load (asset pulled from the Creator Store) or is authored `Looped`
  never fires `Ended`, so music stops for the session.
- Fix: after `Play()`, check `IsLoaded` with a timeout and skip on failure; force `Looped = false`.

### LOB-5. The loading screen preloads every music track

- Where: `ReplicatedFirst/Loading.client.luau` preloads all of `SoundService.Krillion`, including `Music`.
- What happens: several full songs download before the lobby shows, slow on mobile. The only escape is the
  Skip button after 10 s; nothing dismisses the screen if `PreloadAsync` stalls.
- Fix: preload only the SFX children. Add a hard timeout (20 s) that calls `finish()`.

### LOB-6. Shell pickups never despawn and capped players still take them

- Where: `Server/PickupService.luau` `collect` and spawn-spot selection.
- What happens: `collect` destroys the shell even when the player is at `SessionCap`, so a capped player
  silently empties the island for everyone. Shells have no lifetime, and spawn spots accept any
  `Workspace.Lobby` descendant below y 25 (roofs, bunting, lamps, the QuizArc), so unreachable shells can
  fill the `Max = 10` budget and stop restocking (Unconfirmed, depends on geometry).
- Fix: return before `Destroy()` when capped; give shells a lifetime (120 s); pick spots only from walkable
  surfaces (Terrain plus `Lobby.Deck`) or require an upward normal and `CanCollide`.

### LOB-7. Tutorial and quiz replace each other automatically

- Where: `Client/Tutorial.luau` and `Client/LobbyQuiz.luau` through `Windows`.
- What happens: a brand-new player who walks onto the arc while the first-visit tutorial is up has the quiz
  replace it, which fires `TutorialDone` as if they skipped. The reverse: the tutorial's 1.5 s auto-open
  closes the quiz, sends `LobbyQuizStop` and marks it dismissed until they step out and back in.
- Fix: a window that opens automatically (zone or first-show) should wait instead of replacing an open
  window, or the tutorial should treat being replaced as still pending.

## Low

Server
- A player who leaves keeps a live-looking pillar (not marked sunk) with their name on it. Mark it sunk on
  `PlayerRemoving`.
- A revive during Reveal can leave the pillar and the state at different heights: the delayed `risePlatform`
  captured the pre-sink height. Skip the rise when the state height no longer matches, or bump a glide token
  in `restore`.
- The match can start with no humans if everyone leaves or goes AFK during the 0.35 s fade: bots-only match,
  3-2-1, one prompt, Results to nobody. Re-check `MinPlayers` after `introFade`.
- Match prompts exclude only this match's `usedPromptIds`, so a prompt someone just warmed up on can come
  straight back. Also exclude recent lobby-quiz prompts.
- `Net.RoundStatePayload.heights` is declared but never sent or read. Remove it from the type.

Client
- Revive USE celebrates before the server answers; a last-moment tap fails silently and `revived = true`
  stops the Eliminated card returning. Send a `ReviveResult` or wait for `Playing`.
- Waiting shows `PLAYERS 2/2` from `#Players:GetPlayers()` while the server waits on `readyPlayers()`. Send
  a ready count.
- Lobby players get match feedback they cannot act on: danger ticks every second, Tide's camera shake and
  splash at every surge, "EVERYONE STAYED AFLOAT!" with a ping. Gate on `payload.playing`.
- Phone detection differs: Hud uses `TouchEnabled and not KeyboardEnabled`, Status and Toasts use
  `TouchEnabled` alone. A touchscreen laptop gets a mixed layout. Share one `isPhone()`.
- Phone answer bar: the code pins it at 17% from the top, always hides `PromptEcho` and never uses
  `promptText`, while CLAUDE.md says the bar moves above the keyboard and `PromptEcho` repeats the prompt.
  The BoostBar lands in the top-right corner reserved for the player list. Align code and doc; check in the
  phone emulator.
- Dropped answer presses inside `CheckMinInterval`/`RetryCooldown` give no feedback; during someone else's
  overtime the box stays open and every Enter says "TIME'S UP". Nudge on throttle; disable the box when
  `outOfTime`.
- Streak only resets on `round == 1`, so a late joiner's streak carries over from their previous match.
- Each Surge calls `hideCard(false)` without checking `lobbyCard`, so a sunk player's warm-up dive card is
  shrunk away mid-animation at every surge.
- Unconfirmed Studio-side: if `LobbyUi`, `GameUi`, `SharedUi` or the authored `LoadingScreen` have
  `ResetOnSpawn = true`, a reset replaces them and every cached reference dies. Sounds "Wrong" and "Rise" are
  not in `Sounds.model.json`; `Sfx` silently does nothing for a missing name. `Arena.Water` and
  `WaterSurface` are cached by WaterFx, safe only if they never stream out.

Economy and data
- `ProfileRequest` and `SetSetting` have no rate limit; each rebuilds and sends the full payload. Add a 1 s
  per-player cooldown and coalesce `changed` with `task.defer`.
- A refused shop buy is silent after CONFIRM. Fire a `ShopResult {status}` and toast it like `ChestResult`.
- A chest reveal that times out on the client gives no feedback if the result lands late. Toast "check your
  inventory".
- A chest drop whose `Cosmetics.ById[itemId]` is nil takes Shells and grants nothing (tests guard the config).
  Refund and warn when `item == nil`.
- Featured shop items use the client clock (`os.time()`); use `workspace:GetServerTimeNow()` like
  `Client/Quests`.
- Starter Pack sells only Shells to someone who already owns both items, and the card does not say so.
- Pickup `SessionCap` and `ChestSessionLimit` reset on rejoin; pickups are collected by `Touched` with no
  distance check. Make the pickup cap a per-UTC-day profile field if it matters.
- `play_seconds` quest counts AFK time. Skip players with the `Afk` attribute.
- The Wins leaderboard `SetAsync` is not retried; a failure leaves the board stale until the next win.
- An answer payout is lost if the player leaves during `PayoutDelaySeconds` (by design, noted).

Lobby and ambience
- `SeaLife` `swimmers`/`known` and `BillboardScale` entries are never pruned on stream-out; slow growth over
  a long session. Prune on `not IsDescendantOf(workspace)`.
- `DayNight` scans only direct children for Light/Fire/ParticleEmitter and ignores Models tagged
  `NightLight` (Unconfirmed for the authored torches). Use `GetDescendants()` and accept Models.
- Shell bob phase comes from the `GetChildren()` index, so every shell jumps when one is collected. Derive
  it from a stable attribute; make the template Atomic.
- The lobby quiz stays open and payable under the black intro fade (`Playing` is set after the fade). Stop
  quizzes for MatchIntro recipients in `introFade`.
- `SettingsService.addTag` yields up to 10 s on `WaitForChild("Head")`; a quick AFK on/off can attach the tag
  after AFK was turned off. Re-check `Afk` after the wait.
- `default.project.json`: `ServerStorage`, `StarterPack`, `StarterPlayerScripts` and `ServerScriptService`
  use `$path` without `$ignoreUnknownInstances`, so Rojo deletes Studio-only children there. Fine today
  (PhysicalAssets, UIArchive, Sonar are not under a `$path` node), but a trap. Add
  `$ignoreUnknownInstances: true` or a CLAUDE.md note.

## Flow and smoothness

- Gap between matches: Results 10 s + Intermission 15 s + fade + Starting 3 s is about 28 s before round 1
  even when everyone is ready. A shorter post-match intermission (around 8 s) would feel snappier; the lobby
  quiz already fills idle time. Opinion, not a bug.
- With one human the 15 s intermission drops you into a match almost at once after joining or returning.

## What is solid

- Persistence design: `Profile.merge` through `UpdateAsync` means a failed load can never overwrite a real
  save, and a failed autosave puts its delta back ahead of newer changes. Receipts are acknowledged only
  after a confirmed save; double Robux buys convert to Shells; a late Revive Now becomes a stocked revive.
- Every Shells, chest, boost, toy and buy remote is validated on the server (types, ids, ownership, price,
  cooldowns, session limits) before anything is paid. The answer tier never reaches the client.
- `PlayerService.placeAndHold`: token-guarded anchor-and-stream with a timeout; no path leaves a player
  anchored. `tryJoin` never yields and does all its own checks.
- Client controllers guard stale async work with tokens everywhere (`feedbackToken`, `revealToken`,
  `fadeToken`, `calloutToken`, `surgeToken`...), and the streaming rule is followed: WaterFx, PillarTags and
  BillboardScale re-read parts instead of caching them.
- `Client/Windows` has a single owner with idempotent close, so window-stacking bugs are structurally hard.
- The lobby quiz server is careful: serve tokens cancel stale serves, the live match prompt is excluded,
  per-player state is cleaned on leave.
